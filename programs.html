Good things to know

Which option should I start with?

If you want ongoing community, live calls, and a steady stream of resources, the membership is the best value by far. If you have specific questions you want answered right now — your hormones, your results, whether peptides make sense for you — the 1:1 session is the fastest path to clarity.

Can I do both?

Absolutely. Many members join the membership for the ongoing support and then book a 1:1 session when they want a deeper personal review. The two work great together.

Is the membership a contract?

No. The membership is month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. No commitments, no hidden fees.

Do I need to know anything about peptides already?

Not at all. The peptide education starts from the beginning and is designed to be accessible and clear — whether you've heard of peptides before or this is the first time.
